## Direction contract

THESIS: A donation is freight, and freight travels with its paperwork. This
surface owns the consignment document: what arrived, how much, how long it
lives, and where it goes next, stamped. It refuses the arrangement this
category always ships, a soft green charity page with a leaf, white cards and
an "impact" headline, and it refuses that page's opposite, a dark analytics
dashboard with neon chart cards.

OWN-WORLD: Form paper. A manila-kraft ground carries the whole surface, not
an accent on white. Data sits on white form stock inside ruled boxes with
hairline rules and box numerals. Ink is carbon black. One signal red belongs
to the rubber stamp and to the single item that expires first, and to nothing
else. A faded carbon blue is the second ink, for duplicate copies and for
quiet labels. Display type is stamped placard caps. Every figure, code, kg,
and day count is set in typewriter mono, because a number on a form is
typed, not laid out. Controls are parts of the document: a box you tick, a
stub you tear, a stamp you press. Empty states show the ruled empty box, not
blank space.

STORY: The visitor understands in one viewport that this reads a photo of a
donation and returns a decision with its arithmetic shown. They believe it
because the reason line is checkable, not because it is confident. They act
by snapping or pasting a donation on the spot.

FIRST VIEWPORT: One consignment document, seen once, at one scale. Top left,
a stamped placard masthead with the kitchen's name and the live date. The
upper two thirds is the live waybill: a ruled table of four real donation
lines, each with name, qty, kg, storage, days left, and a decision stamped in
the right column, the soonest-expiring line carrying the red. Directly under
it, in the document's own footer, the primary action sits as a working intake
control: a file input styled as the document's own attachment box plus a
paste field, labelled to start a new consignment, not a decorative link to
another page. A perforated tear line closes the viewport, and the section
below begins as the next form, not as more painting.

FORM: The waybill and consignment tag that travels with freight. Candidate 6
of 7 on my grounded list, ordered by resonance. Seed key 1a71f8b1, direction
scope, persuade mode. Four raises, each named for the challenger that donated
it:

- RAISE, from the chaekgeori reverse-perspective shelf (declined): total
  commitment of the container. Every storage reading is a ruled box, and an
  empty box shows the ruled form rather than blank space.
- RAISE, from the window-box toy shelf (declined): one object carries both
  summary and detail. An item line tears open in place to show its reason
  and its redirect, with no navigation away.
- RAISE, from hand-processed film bloom (declined): wear is bound to the
  event, never applied as a page filter. Stamp and ink wear appear only on
  the item whose day count is lowest.
- RAISE, from the night-flight six-pack (competitive, held product clarity):
  a fixed constellation where each panel owns exactly one truth, read in a
  set order, and a deviation shows as a trend before it shows as an alarm.
  Free kg per storage type reads as three fixed gauges that move, not as
  three numbers that appear.

Carried but not built as a world: the algorave's pending-change idea
(competitive). The fit result previews what will happen to free space before
the user commits.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its
provenance
