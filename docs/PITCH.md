# Demonstration and pitch

## Two-minute walkthrough

The companion MP4 explains the scenario with captions and model graphics. Use the sequence below for a screen recording of the actual app.

| Time | Show | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:15 | Intake, reviewed carrot example | A kitchen receives 12 kg of carrots. How much can it put in scheduled meals? |
| 0:15–0:35 | Kitchen fit and ingredient gap | Soup needs 8 kg and the kitchen has 2 kg. RescueFit allocates 6 kg and identifies which adjustable purchase it could replace. |
| 0:35–0:58 | Redirect map and alternate | Show the other 6 kg queued. Mark a destination unavailable, review the new candidate and then restore availability for the saved plan. |
| 0:58–1:19 | Use-first plan and handoff | Save the plan. Returning to the map, confirm the recipient and record the handoff. Back in the plan, show that food use is a separate event. |
| 1:19–1:51 | Impact lab | Run No alternates and compare with the saved reference numbers. Show used and unallocated kg together. Step through two replay days. |
| 1:51–2:00 | Data sources | Point to the PEI report's historical mix. Ask for one kitchen to test a real decision. |

## Seven-minute finalist deck outline

1. **The receiving decision, 0:00–0:45.** Introduce the collected lot and the planned meal gap.
2. **PEI context and prior work, 0:45–1:30.** Explain what the historical report and consultation cover and where the proposed workflow begins.
3. **Prototype walkthrough, 1:30–3:40.** Move one lot from intake through fit to the recorded handoff and distinct use record.
4. **Scenario design and findings, 3:40–5:05.** Compare the same period across three policies. Include increased unallocated mass and the no-alternate case.
5. **Adoption and costs, 5:05–6:10.** Outline the one-kitchen shadow pilot and the effort it would measure.
6. **Next test, 6:10–7:00.** Ask for a reconstructed event and partner participation, then keep the model settings available for questions.
