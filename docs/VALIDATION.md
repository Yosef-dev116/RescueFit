# Operator review and pilot

Start with one partner that receives edible ingredients and prepares meals. Check that the partner can change future purchases, accept part of an offer or propose an onward handoff.

## Reconstruct a recent case

1. Ask what the kitchen received or was offered. Capture known weight, approved handling window, meals, stock, staffing and purchases at the time.
2. Record the choice staff made before showing the prototype. Ask which parts of that decision could still have changed.
3. Enter both decisions against the same inputs. Compare achievable use, purchases that could be adjusted, added effort, transport and unresolved weight.
4. Identify uncertainties and differences. Label observations, operator reports, estimates and assumptions separately.

One case gives evidence of usability and plausibility. A follow-up trial is needed to estimate repeatable savings. Get permission before publishing operational details or a quote.

## Four-week pilot outline

- **Week 1:** Reconstruct initial decisions and record the ordinary process. Measure median staff entry time and distinguish held, used and discarded kg.
- **Weeks 2 and 3:** Run RescueFit alongside the current workflow. Staff review and can override every suggested action. Record every handoff confirmation and actual receipt.
- **Week 4:** Review net adjustable purchasing after added direct costs, kg used, expiry, unallocated weight, quantities served, availability and staff time. Review reasons the recommendation was rejected.

Proceed only if the kitchen finds an actionable repeat use case that holds up after added effort. Use standard handling and escalation procedures set by the organization.
