# Adoption and growth

**First user:** A community or institutional meal kitchen with predictable meal services and recurring eligible offers. An intake lead or kitchen coordinator verifies weights, purchase flexibility and existing stock.

**Initial sponsor:** Test support through a food recovery partner, institution or grant. Ask that sponsor about the support burden, value of documented outcomes and overlap with its current tools. Keep the early receiving pilot free while gathering evidence on setup and staff time.

**Costs:** Estimate staff onboarding, approved recipe maintenance, shared storage and access controls, coordination, and hosting. The offline prototype has no model API charge. If a later model feature is added, measure its cost against time saved.

**Expansion:** Begin with one kitchen. Add a small group only if a willing partner can maintain receiving calendars, capacity and confirmation. Measure failed handoffs and share outcomes with the pilot partner. Province-wide and Atlantic expansion would follow those checks.

**Further revenue:** A supported integration or reporting service could be tested with institutions or donors. Tax documentation, willingness to pay and market size require separate work. The PEI business-count table is one sizing input and does not represent the addressable number of customers.
